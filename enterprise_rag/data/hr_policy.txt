SECTION 1 - LEAVE POLICY

Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued at 1.5 days per month. Leave must be applied for at least 5 working days in
advance through the internal HR portal (hr.meridian.internal). Unused leave of up to
10 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days
will lapse on March 31 of the following year.

Sick leave is capped at 14 days per year and requires a medical certificate for
absences exceeding 2 consecutive days. Maternity leave follows statutory guidelines
(16 weeks for Singapore-based employees). Paternity leave is 2 weeks, to be taken
within 12 months of the child's birth.

Emergency or compassionate leave (up to 3 days) may be granted at the discretion of
the department head and HR, and does not count against annual leave balance.

SECTION 2 - CODE OF CONDUCT

All Meridian employees are expected to uphold the highest standards of professional
behaviour, both within the office and when representing the company externally.
This includes but is not limited to:

- Treating all colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect regardless of seniority,
  gender, nationality, or background.
- Maintaining confidentiality of proprietary company information, client data, and
  internal business strategies. Any breach of confidentiality may result in immediate
  termination and legal action.
- Avoiding conflicts of interest. Employees must disclose any personal or financial
  relationships with vendors or clients to the compliance team.
- Zero tolerance for workplace harassment, discrimination, or bullying. Incidents
  should be reported to hr@meridian.internal or via the anonymous grievance portal.

Violations of the Code of Conduct are reviewed by the HR committee and may result
in verbal warnings, written warnings, suspension, or termination depending on severity.

---

SECTION 3 - REMOTE WORK POLICY

Meridian supports a hybrid work arrangement. Employees in roles classified as
"remote-eligible" may work from home up to 3 days per week, subject to manager
approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM (local time) during which
all employees are expected to be reachable.

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a secure and productive
work environment. Use of personal devices for accessing company systems is permitted
only through the approved VPN client. Sharing of login credentials is strictly
prohibited.

Remote work arrangements may be revoked at management's discretion if performance,
communication, or security standards are not maintained.
