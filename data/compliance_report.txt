MERIDIAN TECHNOLOGIES PTE. LTD.
Internal Compliance Summary - Q4 2024 / January 2025 Review
Classification: Internal Use Only
Prepared by: Aisha Kamara, Compliance Officer

---

OVERVIEW

This report summarises Meridian's current compliance posture against ISO 27001
(Information Security Management) and GDPR obligations for the period ending
31 December 2024. Overall, the organisation remains compliant with no critical
findings. Two medium-severity observations have been raised and are being tracked
for remediation.

---

ISO 27001 STATUS

The annual internal audit conducted in November 2024 confirmed that core controls
across access management, incident response, and asset classification remain
operational and effective. Staff completed mandatory security awareness training
with a completion rate of 94% (target: 100%). The remaining 6% are in progress
and are expected to complete by end of January 2025.

One medium observation was noted: privileged access reviews for the analytics_db
server were last conducted in July 2024, exceeding the 6-month review cycle. A
review has been scheduled for 20 January 2025.

---

GDPR COMPLIANCE

All personal data processing activities are documented in the Records of Processing
Activities (RoPA) register, last updated October 2024. Meridian received 3 data
subject access requests (DSARs) in Q4 2024, all resolved within the statutory 30-day
window. No personal data breaches were reported in this period.

A second medium observation relates to the retention of employee applicant data.
Candidate records older than 12 months were identified in the recruitment database
and have not yet been purged. The data team has been notified and deletion is
scheduled for 31 January 2025.
